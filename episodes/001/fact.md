# Episode 001: Why does Heinz ketchup say 57?

**Object:** the ketchup bottle on almost every table.
**Hook:** "Hey Dex... why does Heinz ketchup say 57 on the bottle?"
**Kip's wrong theory:** 57 tomatoes squished into every bottle.
**Reveal:** the number is basically made up: a marketing idea from a train ad.
**Second payoff:** on glass bottles, the 57 is where Heinz says to tap.
**Loop:** Kip ends by asking "why 57?" again, which runs straight back into line 1.

Glass bottles only; the script says so |

## Wording guardrails

- Don't say "every ketchup bottle". The 57 is Heinz's.
- Don't claim the lucky-numbers story as fact.
- The tap tip applies to **glass** bottles (squeeze bottles don't need it).
- Props are a generic ketchup bottle: no Heinz logo or keystone label.
